Encode a word

Each letter gets its own random prime, raised to the letter's number. The encoded number is the product of all of them.

Letters A–Z only. No spaces or punctuation.
Primes come from your browser's cryptographic random generator and are checked with Miller–Rabin.

The primes are random, so the number keeps which letters you used but not their order. The decoder recovers the letters and then searches for every word they can spell.

Decode a number

Factors the number into primes, turns each exponent back into a letter, then searches the dictionary, names, places, phrases and every arrangement of the letters.

Spaces, commas and line breaks are ignored.
